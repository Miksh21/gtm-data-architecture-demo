# Operating rhythm at 20 hours a week

About 4 hours every working day, so there is presence daily and calls fit any day.

## Weekly

| Day | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | Pipeline review with the Head of Commercial (30 min): last week's numbers, accounts moving tiers, decisions needed. Plan the week. |
| Tuesday | Account research and messaging for tier A accounts. |
| Wednesday | Discovery calls and follow-ups. |
| Thursday | Discovery calls; list and signal upkeep (new signals in, stale ones out). |
| Friday | Written weekly report; one improvement to the system (a new signal, a better template, a fixed data gap). |

## What the Head of Commercial gets, and when

- Monday: one-page pipeline view: new tier A accounts, meetings booked, replies, deals opened, with the signal behind each.
- Within 24 hours: notes after every discovery call, qualified against the four criteria.
- Monthly: what worked and what did not, by signal and by persona, and what changes next month.

## First 30 days

1. Weeks 1 to 2: review the last 10 to 15 won deals (source, referrer, buyer title, trigger, time to close). Rewrite hypotheses, signals and personas to match.
2. Week 3: data model and scoring live on the real account list; first 20 tier A accounts.
3. Week 4: first messages out, first calls; first monthly readout.

## Working alongside other engagements

Fixed hours, the same every day, calls booked into shared calendar slots. Everything lives in shared systems (CRM, this repo, the weekly report), so nothing depends on me being online.
