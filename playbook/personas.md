# Personas: who gets the message and why

The signal tells us when to reach out. The persona decides who receives it. Each hypothesis has one primary persona (owns the problem), one economic buyer (signs) and one fallback.

## H1: vertical SaaS sitting on data its customers would pay for

| Role | Typical titles | Why them | Opening angle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | VP / Head of Product, CPO | Owns the reporting roadmap and the decision to sell insights as a paid tier | "Which insights do your customers actually pay for?" |
| Economic buyer | CEO or CRO (smaller firms), CPO (larger) | Paid add-on = new revenue line, so it sits with whoever owns revenue | Revenue from data already in the platform |
| Fallback | Head of Data / Data Engineering | Feels the build capacity gap first | Build capacity vs. knowing what to build |

Do not contact the person in the open role. They are not hired yet. Write to their future manager, the one named in the job post.

Owner.com example: the Principal PM for Reporting reports into product leadership, so the message goes to the CPO or VP Product, not to recruiting and not to the role itself.

## H2: companies in their first year under a PE owner

| Role | Typical titles | Why them | Opening angle |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Operating Partner / Value Creation at the fund | One relationship opens several portfolio companies; closest to a referral motion | Board reporting across the portfolio in the first 100 days |
| Economic buyer | CFO of the portfolio company | Owns board reporting and the data stack after the deal | One set of numbers from many acquired systems |
| Fallback | CEO of the portfolio company | Under pressure to show the value creation plan | Time to first reliable board pack |

Timing check: a new CFO after the deal is the second signal. Without it, wait.

## Rules

- One persona per message. If two people get a message, each gets a different angle.
- The persona is decided before the copy is written, never after.
- After the first won-deal review, replace these with the titles that actually appear on won deals.
