# Qualification

Four criteria, asked as open questions on the first call. Each answer is logged on the deal so later we can see which answers predicted a win.

| Criterion | H1 question | H2 question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Budget | Is reporting funded as core platform work, or does it have to earn its keep as a paid tier? Who owns that number? | Is the data work in the value creation plan budget, or does it compete with other initiatives? |
| Fit | Which data is already clean and joined, and is the gap build capacity or knowing what customers will pay for? | How many systems came in with the acquisitions, and which numbers does the board not trust yet? |
| Timing | What has to ship before the new hire starts, and is there a launch or pricing date in the next two quarters? | When is the next board pack, and what must be in it that is not there now? |
| Who decides | Who signs off on an outside partner: CPO, CTO or CEO? | Does the fund approve outside partners, or the portfolio CFO? |

Disqualify early if: no owner for the number, no date in the next two quarters, or the data does not exist yet.
